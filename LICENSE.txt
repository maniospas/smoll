introduction
============
As a small programming language, smoλ (smoll) aims to be compatible with
practical development needs, while retaining attribution rights to the
core of the language.

My goal is to let anyone include the standard library in their source
code, as well as reuse segments of the test suite, without infringing
on any copyright. But derivative works and redistribution of the smoll
transpiler should retain attribution and this license, as should
redistributions of antcc for the relevant segments.
